Our Practice

DOM Consultants works with founder-led and privately held small businesses undergoing operational transition, whether formalizing early growth, modernizing legacy systems, launching new digital revenue models, or expanding into new divisions.

The practice focuses on the design and implementation of internal systems that support durable performance. Engagements include operational restructuring, departmental development, subscription and digital product structuring, financial performance analysis, and management framework design.

No two businesses arrive at the same place for the same reasons. DOM Consultants meets owners across healthcare, finance, technology, and education exactly where they are, and builds from there.
